# Wish List

A single person's Christmas wish list, published as a read-only page for gift-givers to browse.

## Language

**Wish List**:
The one published list of Wishes belonging to its owner. There is exactly one, and it is edited in place year to year. Gift-givers can only read it; nothing on it records whether a Wish has been bought.
_Avoid_: Gift list, registry

**Wish**:
One thing the owner would like to receive. It always has a name and may also have an image, a purchase link, a Note, and be a Really Want. Wishes appear in the order the owner wrote them.
_Avoid_: Item, gift, present

**Really Want**:
A Wish the owner especially wants. A Wish either is a Really Want or isn't; there are no other priority levels.
_Avoid_: Top pick, priority, favorite

**Note**:
The owner's own words to gift-givers about one Wish, such as which size or colour, or that any brand will do. A Wish has at most one Note, and it may run to several paragraphs.
_Avoid_: Description, subtitle, comment
